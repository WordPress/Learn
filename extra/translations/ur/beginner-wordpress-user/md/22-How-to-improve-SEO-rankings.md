| 17 | The next topic that we want to discuss is metadata. Metadata are tags in your page content that are visible to search engines. | 19 | Next, site speed is essential. Search engines definitely look at site speed when ranking sites, so this is an important part of SEO. | 20 | _[heading]_<br>Image optimization | _[heading]_<br>تصاویر کو بہتر بنانا |
